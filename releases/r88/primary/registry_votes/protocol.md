# Membership in registration examples

The registry tables record exactly the original 75 registration IDs and ordered revised-set bit masks per task. The five positions for each retrieved prompt come from its frozen registration_indices field. Compact verification computes membership in all registered sets, membership among the five actually shown, and agreement with first-example and plurality votes. A plurality tie goes to the earlier retrieved example. Agreement with a vote is descriptive and does not establish prompt-level copying. No query labels enter retrieval or the membership test. Outcomes use the original candidate/registry partition. Overlap compares soft mapping and primary LPR on the identical query IDs. The union of their correct requests is a post-hoc oracle.
